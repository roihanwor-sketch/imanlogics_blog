## Empirical Discovery & Archaeological Investigation

Accelerator Mass Spectrometry (AMS) radiocarbon testing conducted by the University of Oxford on the Mingana parchment folios yielded a 95.4% probability range of 568–645 CE, precisely overlapping with the prophetic timeline of Prophet Muhammad.

### Academic Interpretation & Paleographical Assessment

International paleographers confirm the Hijazi script on the folios matches standard contemporary Qur'anic text, decisively refuting 20th-century revisionist theories claiming late canonization.

### Qur'anic Scriptural Evidence & Classical Exegesis

In sacred scripture, explicit textual articulation is recorded in **Qur'an 15:9 [15]: 9**:

> **"إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا الذِّكْرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُ لَحَافِظُونَ"**
>
> _Translation: "Indeed, it is We who sent down the Qur'an and indeed, We will be its guardian."_

Classical exegete Ibn Kathir notes this divine safeguard operates through both continuous mass oral memorization (mutawatir) and meticulous early physical inscriptional records.

### Rational Synthesis & Epistemological Conclusion

The exact convergence of nuclear spectrometry physical testing and classical Islamic historiography demonstrates that early Islamic textual transmission adheres to the highest standard of historical reliability in the ancient world.
