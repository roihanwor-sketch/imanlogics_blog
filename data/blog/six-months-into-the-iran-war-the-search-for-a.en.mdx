Authentic faith in Islam is not blind dogma, but conviction arising from contemplation and coherent evidence.

### V. Evidence Matrix & Epistemological Demarcation

- **[FACT]** The Qur'an consistently directs human intellect toward examining creation and verifying claims logically. *(Source: [Al-Qur'an Surah Fussilat: 53](https://quran.ksu.edu.sa))*

- **[EVIDENCE]** Hadith textual and chain-of-transmission criticism represents an early institutional methodology of scientific data verification. *(Source: [Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah (Ulum al-Hadith)](https://shamela.ws))*

- **[COUNTERARGUMENT]** Radical skepticism dismissing all metaphysical grounds fails to account for the objective intelligibility of cosmic laws. *(Source: [Tahafut al-Falasifah (Al-Ghazali) / Yaqeen Institute](https://yaqeeninstitute.org))*

- **[UNCERTAINTY]** Human comprehension of transcendent metaphysical realities remains bounded by empirical observational limits. *(Source: [Dar Ta'arud al-Aql wa al-Naql (Ibn Taymiyyah)](https://shamela.ws))*
