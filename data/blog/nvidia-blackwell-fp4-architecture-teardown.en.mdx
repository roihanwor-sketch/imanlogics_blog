### II. Hardware Deconstruction & Silicon Engineering

Understanding how this performance is physically achieved on semiconductor wafers:

- **Physical Silicon Specifications:** Dual reticle-limited monolithic dies manufactured on TSMC 4NP custom process, fused via advanced CoWoS-L packaging.
- **Microarchitectural Innovations:** 5th-Gen Tensor Cores integrate hardware Decompression Engines and Micro-Tensor Scaling to preserve 4-bit representation fidelity.
- **Thermal & Power Profile:** TDP scales up to 1,200W per socket, mandating direct-to-chip liquid cooling architectures in GB200 NVL72 racks.

#### Deep Dive: FP4 Quantization Mechanics
- **Theoretical Compute Throughput:** 20 PFLOPS FP4 per GPU
- **Quantization Trade-offs:** Utilizes E2M1 microscaling format standardizing scale factors per 16-element block to prevent extreme gradient loss.
- **Empirical Accuracy Impact:** Independent evaluations on Llama 3 70B show perplexity degradation of less than 0.8% compared to native FP16.
